Senior Thesis: Controllable Image Synthesis

Improving Controllability in Image Synthesis using Structural Conditioning (Sketches) & Stable Cascade

Generative diffusion models often suffer from unpredictable composition when driven solely by text prompts. This year-long independent study investigated how structural conditioning via real-time sketches and edge maps constraints Stable Cascade to follow precise user intent.

  • Pipeline Architecture: Engineered a human-in-the-loop drawing-to-image workflow using ml5.js and extended ComfyUI with StreamDiffusion for sub-second inference.
  • Evaluation: Conducted empirical HCI tests evaluating edge-detection fidelity and latency trade-offs.
  • Honours: Awarded Top 3 Senior Thesis by the Wooster CS Department; manuscript archived in the College Repository.
